# Atlas on your phone

Your phone gets its own copy of Atlas. It **works offline** and **syncs with your computer** whenever both are on the same network (home Wi-Fi, a new flat, or your phone's hotspot) and Atlas is open on the computer. Nothing goes through the internet.

**You'll need:** Atlas installed on your computer, and your phone on the same Wi-Fi.

## On your computer
1. Open Atlas and go to **Settings & data → Atlas on your phone**.
2. Tick **Phone sync on**. If your computer asks whether Atlas can accept incoming connections, choose **Allow**.
3. Click **Connect a phone**. A QR code and a **6-digit pairing code** appear. Keep this window open.

## iPhone (use Safari for setup)
> On iPhone only **Safari** can install the security certificate. If your camera opens the link in Chrome, copy the address and paste it into Safari.

1. Scan the QR code with the **Camera** and open the link in **Safari**.
2. **Step 1:** tap **Download certificate**, then **Allow**, then **Close**.
3. Open **Settings**. Tap **Profile Downloaded** near the top (or *General → VPN & Device Management*), then **Install**.
4. Go to **Settings → General → About → Certificate Trust Settings** and switch on **Atlas Local CA**.
5. Back in Safari, step 3 turns green. Tap **Open Atlas**, then **Share → Add to Home Screen → Add**.
6. Open **Atlas from your home screen**. If it asks for a code, type the **6-digit code** shown on your computer.

## Android (Chrome)
1. Scan the QR code and open the link.
2. Tap **Download certificate**. Then open **Settings → Security → Encryption & credentials → Install a certificate → CA certificate** and choose the downloaded *Atlas-Local-CA* file.
3. Back on the page, tap **Open Atlas**, then **Install app** (or ⋮ → **Add to Home screen**).
4. Open Atlas and enter the **6-digit code** if asked.

## Day to day
- The badge at the top shows **Synced · 14:05** or **3 changes waiting**. Tap it to sync straight away.
- Changes made on both devices while apart are merged, so nothing is lost. Edits to different parts of the same item are both kept.
- **More** (bottom right) opens every section. The **+** button quick-adds a task, money in/out, a bill, a journal note or a money move.

## Moving house, new Wi-Fi or your phone's hotspot
You don't need to set anything up again. Atlas on your phone remembers your computer and finds it on whatever network you're both on: a new home Wi-Fi, a café, or your phone's **Personal Hotspot** with the computer connected to it. Open Atlas on the phone and it reconnects by itself within a few seconds.

## Troubleshooting
| Problem | Fix |
|---|---|
| The setup page won't load | The phone must be on the same Wi-Fi (not mobile data or a guest network). Turn off VPNs. Try the number address shown under the QR code. |
| No "Profile Downloaded" | You used Chrome on iPhone. Use Safari for the setup page. The download also expires after about 8 minutes, so download it again. |
| "Code expired" or "too many wrong codes" | Click **Connect a phone** on the computer for a new code. Codes last 30 minutes. |
| Phone says "Can't reach your Mac" | Atlas must be open on the computer, the computer awake, and both on the same network. Your changes wait on the phone and sync later. |
| Remove a phone | On the computer: **Settings → Atlas on your phone → Disconnect**. |
