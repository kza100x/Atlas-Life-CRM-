# Atlas on your phone

Your phone gets its own copy of Atlas. It **works offline** and **syncs with your computer** whenever Atlas is open on the computer and both devices have an internet connection, on any network: home Wi-Fi, mobile data, a café or a new flat.

Your data goes **straight from one device to the other, encrypted**. It isn't stored online anywhere. The internet is only used for the two devices to find each other.

**You'll need:** Atlas 2.8 or newer installed on your computer, and Safari (iPhone) or Chrome (Android). No certificate or profile to install.

## 1. On your computer
1. Open Atlas and go to **Settings & data → Atlas on your phone**.
2. Tick **Phone sync on**.
3. Click **Connect a phone**. A QR code and a code like `5ZM2-PXTP-QZPL-RZKZ` appear. Keep this window open until the phone says it's connected.

## 2a. iPhone
1. Point the **Camera** at the QR code and tap the link. It opens in Safari or Chrome.
2. Tap **Share** (the square with an arrow), then **Add to Home Screen**, then **Add**.
3. Open **Atlas from your home screen**. It connects to your computer and copies your data across.
   - If it shows **Connect to your computer** instead, tap **Scan the code** and point it at the QR code on your computer, or type the code.

## 2b. Android
1. Scan the QR code with the camera (or Google Lens) and open the link in **Chrome**. Atlas connects and copies your data across.
2. Tap **Install** when Chrome offers it (or **⋮ → Add to Home screen → Install**).

**No QR scanner?** On the phone, open **kza100x.github.io/Atlas-Life-CRM-** and type the code shown on your computer.

## Day to day
- The badge at the top shows **Synced · 14:05** or **3 changes waiting**. Tap it to sync straight away.
- Changes made on both devices while apart are merged, so nothing is lost. Edits to different parts of the same item are both kept.
- **More** (bottom right) opens every section, including **Businesses → New business**. The **+** button quick-adds a task, money in/out, a bill, a journal note, a money move or a business.
- Moving house, travelling or using mobile data doesn't matter. As long as both devices are online and Atlas is open on the computer, they find each other.

## Troubleshooting
| Problem | Fix |
|---|---|
| "Your computer didn't answer" | Atlas must be open on the computer (it can be in the background) and the computer awake and online. On the computer, **Settings → Atlas on your phone** should say **Ready for your phones**. |
| "That code isn't active" | Codes work once and last 30 minutes. Click **Connect a phone** on the computer for a new one. |
| Syncs at home but not on mobile data | Some mobile and workplace networks block direct connections between devices. Your changes wait safely on the phone and sync as soon as you're on Wi-Fi again. |
| The phone says **Disconnected** | It was removed on the computer. In the phone app go to **Settings → This phone → Disconnect this phone**, then connect again. |
| Remove a phone | On the computer: **Settings → Atlas on your phone → Disconnect**. |
| No internet at all where you are | On the computer, click **Connect a phone → Connect over Wi-Fi only**. This older method works without internet but needs a security certificate on the phone. |

## Already using the Wi-Fi version from 2.7?
It keeps working. To switch to the new version (no certificate, works anywhere), delete the old Atlas icon from your home screen and follow the steps above. Your data comes back from your computer. Sync any waiting changes first.
